# Top 5 Strongest Hindu Gods — narration script

Who is the strongest Hindu god? Here are five you never want to stand against.

Number five: Hanuman. When Lakshmana lay dying, Hanuman flew to the Himalayas for one healing herb. He couldn't find it, so he tore off the whole mountain and carried it back.

Number four: Indra, king of the heavens. The serpent Vritra swallowed the world's rivers. Indra struck him down with the Vajra, his thunderbolt, and set the waters free.

Number three: Durga. The buffalo demon Mahishasura could not be killed by any god. So the gods poured their power into one warrior. Ten arms, ten weapons, riding a lion. She ended him.

Number two: Vishnu, the preserver. As the dwarf Vamana, he asked a demon king for three steps of land. Then he grew until his three steps covered the earth, the sky, and everything beyond.

And number one: Shiva, the destroyer. One glance from his third eye burned the god of desire to ash. And when the universe ends, it ends in his dance.

To many devotees, Shiva, Vishnu or the Goddess is the supreme power above all. Who's your number one?
